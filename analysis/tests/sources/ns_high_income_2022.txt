HIGH INCOME TRENDS AND EFFECTIVE TAX RATES, 2022
In Canada, those reporting total income (including capital gains) over $322,000 in 2022 represented the top 1 per cent of taxfilers.
The top 10% of taxfilers in Canada had total income (including capital gains) of $115,000.
The top 1% of earners in Canada reported 12.6% of the total income (including capital gains) in 2022 and paid 22.7% of Federal and Provincial income taxes.
Across Canada, the effective tax rate was 20.1% in 2022.
Statistics Canada. Table 11-10-0054-01; Table 11-10-0056-01
